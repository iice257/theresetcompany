# Tibo's 28-Day Codex Sprint — collated (Oct 4 → Nov 1, 2026 · 29 calendar days)

**Who:** Tibo = Thibault Sottiaux (@thsottiaux), leads Codex & ChatGPT Work at OpenAI. ~975K followers.
**Rival:** lauren (@poteto), engineer at SpaceXAI/xAI (ex-React compiler), ships "bots" (Grok bot) and the `pstack` skills.
**The rule (Oct 4):** *"Over the next 28 days, each day we'll either ship one thing that is a clear improvement and relevant for most codex/work users or ship a full reset."*
**Final tally (verified):** 15 ship days (1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, 16, 19, 22, 24, 25, 26) and 13 reset days (2, 6, 8, 10, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 27, 28), of which 27 and 28 were banked. Day 3 was a ship day that also loaded a bonus banked reset. Days 2 and 18 shipped things and *still* reset. Days are Pacific calendar days.

> Why 29 days: kickoff on Oct 4 (Day 0) plus 28 numbered days (Oct 5 to Nov 1). Poteto also borrowed his format for the finale: *"Day 28/ tibo's game has landed."*
